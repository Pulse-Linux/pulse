Verifying Checksums

Always verify your ISO download against our SHA256 checksums before flashing to a USB or installing on bare metal.

# For Linux & macOS (bash/zsh):
sha256sum pulse-linux-1.0-gnome-amd64.iso
# For Windows PowerShell:
Get-FileHash pulse-linux-1.0-gnome-amd64.iso -Algorithm SHA256
